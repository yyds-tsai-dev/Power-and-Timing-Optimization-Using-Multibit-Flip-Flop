\section{Exactly Priced Refinement Operators}\label{sec:operators}

The oracle of Section~\ref{sec:oracle} turns refinement into a pricing problem: any candidate move can be quoted its exact change in official cost before it is committed. The engine comprises three exactly-priced move classes---bit re-pairing, structural rebanking, and bin-density eviction---plus two positional operators inherited from the reference flow (relocation and critical-pair swap). The exactly-priced classes share one contract: a candidate is screened with a side-effect-free delta, re-priced exactly against the committed state at apply time, and accepted only if the full weighted delta $\alpha\,\Delta\tns + \beta\,\Delta P + \gamma\,\Delta A + \lambda\,\Delta V$ is negative, so every committed sequence is monotone decreasing in the official cost. The positional operators accept on the exact $\tns$ delta at fixed power and area, without pricing the bin term, so $V$ can drift during relocation; the density stage that closes the schedule re-prices every remaining violation explicitly.

\subsection{Batch Bit Re-Pairing}\label{sec:bitrepair}

The finest-grained move exchanges individual bits between two multi-bit cells, or permutes bit-to-slot assignments within one cell. The search is a batched best-improvement local search in the spirit of multi-move-per-round methods from scheduling~\cite{congram2002dynasearch}: each round enumerates the best swap for every cell in a candidate neighborhood, then commits many improving swaps together. Candidate evaluation is embarrassingly parallel because the screening delta touches no shared mutable state.

Exact pricing changes what a batch may contain. Conventional batched local search commits only conflict-disjoint candidates, since overlapping timing cones invalidate screened gains; under exact re-pricing each survivor is re-quoted against the committed state immediately before it is applied, so a stale gain can only cause a rejection, never a bad commit. The inherited flow's conservative disjoint batching committed roughly $10\%$ of improving candidates per round; dropping the disjointness filter commits every candidate that remains improving at apply time, an order-of-magnitude increase in per-round throughput at identical safety.

Intra-cell slot permutation complements inter-cell exchange: because the evaluator prices each sink pin's displacement individually, permuting which bit occupies which physical slot changes timing cost without moving the cell. These moves are priced by the same delta evaluator and folded into the same batches. Periodic full rescans re-enumerate the entire move space and reseed the candidate queue; between rescans the engine drains cheap dirty-set candidates, and each rescan certifies that no improving move was orphaned by earlier commits.

\subsection{Oracle-Guided Structural Rebanking}\label{sec:rebank}

Structural rebanking changes the banking solution itself after legalization, merging $2{\times}2\mathrm{b}{\to}4\mathrm{b}$ and $4{\times}1\mathrm{b}{\to}4\mathrm{b}$. Candidates are enumerated per cell from its same-clock $k$-nearest neighbors, so enumeration cost stays linear in design size.

Each candidate passes through two phases. A parallel screening phase prices the merge with the group-move oracle at a centroid placement estimate: power, area, and density deltas are exact from the cell library and bin table; the timing delta assumes the merged cell lands at the candidates' centroid. An exact phase then processes survivors in best-first order of screened gain. The merge is staged rather than applied: netlist map mutations are deferred, a legal site is sought for the merged cell, and the oracle re-prices the move at the actual site; if the exact delta is negative the staged state is committed, otherwise rollback restores wiring, pin mapping, and placement exactly. Acceptance is strictly monotone, and a patience cutoff terminates the phase after a fixed number of consecutive rejections.

The same move class cascades catastrophically under one-hop proxy pricing (Sec.~\ref{sec:background}). Under exact pricing it produces strictly monotone descent; on one hidden case each $2{\times}2\mathrm{b}{\to}4\mathrm{b}$ merge alone is worth $-3{,}000$ weighted power-and-area units. The difference lies in the accept pricing and in the staged commit structure that exact pricing makes safe to relax; Sec.~\ref{sec:ablation} separates the two experimentally.

\subsection{Density Pricing and the Interleaved Framework}\label{sec:density}\label{sec:framework}

The density penalty $\lambda \cdot V$ counts bins whose utilization exceeds the limit. Prior refinement operators in this flow could not see the term, so none could trade timing against it. We price it directly, jointly with exact timing. For each violating bin, the operator builds an eviction chain relocating flip-flops from the bin to legal sites outside it. The chain is priced as a unit, $\alpha \sum \Delta\tns + \lambda\,\Delta V$, and committed only if the total is negative; otherwise every eviction is reverted. Chain-level commit-or-revert matters because a violation clears only when the bin drops below the limit, so partial evictions pay timing cost for no penalty reduction.

The operators run as interleaved rounds---relocation, critical-pair swap, bit re-pairing, structural rebanking---repeated twice, with density repair closing the schedule. The two inherited positional operators are retained as move generators but re-priced by the oracle: relocation moves a flip-flop to a legalized site near its slack-weighted driver centroid and accepts only on exact $\tns$ improvement; critical-pair swap exchanges the positions of two same-footprint cells, cost-free in power, area, and legality, so acceptance reduces to the sign of the exact timing delta. Interleaving matters because merges create new re-pairing opportunities and repaired bit assignments expose new profitable merges; on one hidden case the interleaved schedule beat the same operators run as a single pass by $185$K weighted cost units. Each operator exits its round once it converges, and because all structural commits are monotone in the official cost the framework is anytime in practice: interrupting it at any point yields a legal solution, and in all recorded runs the official score descends monotonically across stage boundaries (Fig.~\ref{fig:anytime}).

One repair to the inherited flow determines the move space all operators see. The flow tested a per-cell boolean as a placement-liveness predicate when selecting refinement candidates. Auditing the flag's writers showed it is a work-queue marker for the banking stage; cells placed by the final legalizer keep the stale value forever. On one hidden case the flag silently excluded 2{,}024 of 6{,}309 physical flip-flops ($32\%$) from every refinement operator, including all 2{,}000 two-bit cells---the entire two-bit exchange space of Section~\ref{sec:bitrepair}. We replace every flag test with membership in the placement map, the structure the solution writer and legality checkers treat as ground truth, so refinement operates on the complete move space by construction.
