\section{Introduction}

Multi-bit flip-flop (MBFF) banking replaces groups of single-bit registers with multi-bit trays that share internal clock inverters, cutting clock dynamic power and cell area at the price of placement freedom and timing slack~\cite{chang2010postplacement,shyu2013effective,kahng2016floptray}. The ICCAD 2024 CAD Contest Problem~B~\cite{yang2024contest,fang2024overview} distilled the problem into a reproducible benchmark: banking and placement are optimized jointly under a four-term cost $\alpha\cdot\tns + \beta\cdot\mathrm{Power} + \gamma\cdot\mathrm{Area} + \lambda\cdot\mathrm{Density}$, scored by an official evaluator on seven industrial-scale cases with hard legality constraints.

Banking is a sequence of committed discrete decisions, and nearly every published flow prices those decisions with a proxy timing model. Proxy error does not average out: each mispriced merge is committed, physically moves pins, and distorts the slack landscape that prices every later decision. We measured this compounding directly. Our baseline, a top-ranked open-source implementation of the contest reference flow, carries a one-hop timing proxy that underestimates evaluator TNS by roughly 34\% on the hardest case; under it, cascades of mispriced $2{\times}2\mathrm{b}{\to}4\mathrm{b}$ merges regress the score by up to $+254\%$, and a debank-and-rebank pass by $+134\%$ (Sec.~\ref{sec:background}). The natural reading is that structural rebanking after legalization is too dangerous a move class; our central claim is the opposite: under exact per-move pricing with staged, monotone acceptance it becomes strictly profitable, and a controlled counterfactual (Sec.~\ref{sec:ablation}) puts the price of proxy quotes at up to $0.9\%$ of final score. Pricing, not the move class, is the blocker.

We therefore rebuild the refinement stage around exact pricing. The upstream flow is the open-source baseline we had previously strengthened under the same proxy pricing (Sec.~\ref{sec:background}); those enhancements are not claims of this paper. Everything downstream of detailed placement is the contribution here:

\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{An evaluator-exact, side-effect-free incremental timing oracle.} It prices any candidate move (bit re-pairing, structural rebanking, positional relocation and swaps, and bin-density eviction) against the official cost function without mutating placement state; incremental and full recomputation agree to $0.0$ in every round, and the side-effect-free delta matches a real apply-and-revert to within $7.4\times10^{-13}$.
\item \textbf{Exactly re-priced batch local search.} Conflict-free batching, the standard safety device in batched placement moves, commits about 10\% of improving candidates per round; because every candidate is re-priced exactly at apply time, the disjointness requirement is dropped entirely: a stale quote can only cause a rejection, never a bad commit.
\item \textbf{Monotone-accept structural rebanking with rollback staging.} Merges ($2{\times}2\mathrm{b}{\to}4\mathrm{b}$, $4{\times}1\mathrm{b}{\to}4\mathrm{b}$) are staged outside the committed netlist, priced exactly, committed only on strict improvement, and rolled back completely otherwise.
\item \textbf{Density-term pricing combined with exact timing pricing.} A repair pass evicts flip-flops from violating bins, accepting an eviction chain only if the exact timing plus density delta improves total cost, making all four cost terms visible to refinement.
\end{itemize}

The rebuild also removed a silent move-space restriction in the baseline: a work-queue flag misread as placement liveness excluded 32\% of flip-flops from every refinement operator on one hidden case, including all of its two-bit MBFFs.

These operator classes, run as interleaved rounds and all quoted through one oracle interface, form the framework (Secs.~\ref{sec:oracle}--\ref{sec:operators}). A single configuration, unchanged across all seven benchmarks, beats the best contest top-3 entry on every case, the first published method to do so, with a composite score ratio of 0.953 versus 0.991 for the contest winner's method under the same baseline (a two-page late-breaking result~\cite{li2025lbr}); ours is the first full-length method paper at this quality level on this benchmark (Sec.~\ref{sec:mainresults}). Every solution we report passes the official evaluator and both legality checkers.
