\section{Conclusion}

\looseness=-1 We presented a post-placement MBFF refinement framework whose accepts are priced by a side-effect-free incremental timing oracle. Exact pricing turns structural rebanking from a cascading liability into strictly monotone improvement and raises batch commit throughput by an order of magnitude. The density penalty obeys the same accept rule as timing. One configuration beat the best contest top-3 entry on all seven benchmarks: composite ratio 0.953, against 0.979--0.991 for the best published methods~\cite{li2025lbr,chen2026agile}. Limitations: evidence covers one benchmark family and its cost function, the ideal-clock model excludes clock networks, and tight deadlines would reintroduce truncation variance; future work extends the discipline to debank/eject moves and richer bit widths.
