TITLE:
Exact-Oracle Post-Placement Refinement for Multi-Bit Flip-Flop Banking: Structural Rebanking without Cascades

ABSTRACT (208 words):
Multi-bit flip-flop (MBFF) banking trades clock power and area against timing. Most flows drive it with proxy cost models, and the mispricing compounds: each committed merge distorts the cost of every later decision. On the ICCAD 2024 CAD Contest Problem B benchmark, we show that the root cause is the pricing error rather than the move class. Structural rebanking moves that cascade under a one-hop proxy model (up to +254% score) become strictly profitable once re-priced exactly under staged, monotone acceptance. We present a post-placement refinement framework built on an evaluator-exact, side-effect-free incremental timing oracle. The oracle prices every candidate move in closed loop with the official cost function: bit re-pairing, structural rebanking of 2x2b->4b and 4x1b->4b, positional relocation and swaps, and bin-density eviction. Exact pricing enables (i) batch local search that safely commits an order of magnitude more moves per round than conservative conflict-free batching, (ii) monotone-accept structural rebanking with rollback-safe staging, and (iii) refinement-side pricing of the density penalty term. A single configuration beats the contest top-3 best entry on all seven benchmarks; ours is the first published method to do so. It reaches a composite ratio of 0.953, where the strongest previously published methods reach 0.979--0.991, with every solution verified legal by the official evaluator.

KEYWORDS:
multi-bit flip-flop; banking; incremental timing analysis; detailed placement; local search
