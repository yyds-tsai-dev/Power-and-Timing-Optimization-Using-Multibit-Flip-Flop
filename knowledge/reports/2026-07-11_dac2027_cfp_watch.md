## Research-track format (per DAC 2026 guidelines, expected to carry over)

- Two-stage submission: title/abstract/full author list first; manuscript ~1 week later.
- **6 pages excluding references**, double-column, 9–10 pt font, PDF only, ACM template.
- **Double-blind** review; no significant content revisions allowed post-acceptance beyond formatting/acknowledgements/refs withheld for anonymity.
- One author must register at Research Speaker Rate; ACM holds copyright (ACM Open Access / APC policies apply).
- **AI-disclosure**: no explicit generative-AI/LLM disclosure policy found in the DAC 2026 research manuscript guidelines; the general ACM authorship policy (disclose generative-AI use in text creation; AI cannot be an author) would govern by default. Re-check when the 2027 CFP posts.
